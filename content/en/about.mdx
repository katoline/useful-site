## About Image Adjust

Image Adjust is a free tool for converting, compressing, and optimizing images for websites and landing pages, right in your browser. You can convert between PNG, JPEG, and WebP, compress and resize images, and generate a complete set of favicons and web app icons. There's no sign-up: just open the page and get started.

## How to Use

### Convert & Compress

The [Convert & Compress tool](/) lets you change an image's format and reduce its file size.

1. Add your images. You can pick files, drag and drop them (desktop only), add a whole folder or ZIP file at once, or load an image from a URL.
2. Choose an output format (PNG, JPEG, or WebP) and how you want to compress. You can set the quality with a slider, set a target file size such as "under 200 KB", resize by width, height, or percentage, or combine these.
3. You'll see the before and after sizes and how much you saved. Use the comparison slider to check the result side by side, then download images one at a time or all together as a ZIP.

When you convert an image with transparency to JPEG, you can choose the background color used to fill the transparent areas (white by default). Metadata such as EXIF, including any location data in your photos, is removed by default.

### Icon Generator

The [Icon Generator](/icons/) creates every icon your website needs from a single source image. A square SVG, or a PNG of at least 512 px, works best.

- It exports favicon.ico, PNG icons in several sizes, an Apple Touch Icon, PWA icons (including a maskable icon), a web app manifest, and an Open Graph image, all bundled in a ZIP.
- You can adjust the padding, background color, and corner radius, and preview how the icon looks at 16 px and 32 px and in both light and dark browser tabs.
- It also generates the HTML tags to paste into your site, ready to copy.

For tips on choosing an image format or installing favicons, see our [guides](/guide/).

### Install as an App (PWA)

Image Adjust is a progressive web app (PWA), so you can install it on your phone or computer like a regular app.

- **Android and desktop Chrome or Edge:** Use the install button shown on the page, or choose "Install app" from the browser menu.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Tap the Share button in Safari and choose "Add to Home Screen".

Once installed, the app is stored on your device, so converting, compressing, and generating icons all work even when you're offline. Loading images from a URL and showing ads still require an internet connection.

On Android, you can share an image from your gallery or another app straight to Image Adjust, and it opens in the converter. On desktop Chrome and Edge, you can also open image files with the app directly. When a new version is available, you'll see a notice, and reloading the page switches to it.

### Language and Theme

Use the menu at the top of the page to switch the language (Japanese or English) and the theme (light, dark, or match your device). Switching languages after adding images keeps your images and settings intact. Your language and theme choices are saved on your device, so the site opens the same way next time.

## What Makes Image Adjust Different

### Your Images Are Processed in Your Browser

All converting, compressing, and icon generation happens inside your browser. Images you add are never sent to or stored on our servers, so you can safely work with confidential materials or personal photos without them leaving your device.

The one exception is loading an image from a URL. Browsers often block direct requests for images on other websites (because of CORS restrictions), so when you use this feature, the URL you enter is sent to our relay server, which fetches the image and passes it back to your browser. The relay server doesn't store the image or the URL. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details.

### Free, No Account Needed

Every feature is free and works without an account. The site is supported by the ads shown on its pages.

### Handles Large Images and Batches

You can process many images, folders, or ZIP files in one go. How large an image you can handle depends on your device's memory. If an image is too large for your device, it's skipped with a notice, so try again on a computer or resize it first.
