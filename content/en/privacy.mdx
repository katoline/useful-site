This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Image Adjust ("we", "us", or "the Service") handles information about the people who use it.

## How We Handle Your Images

All converting, compressing, and icon generation in the Service happens inside your browser. The images you add, and the processed results, are never sent to or stored on our servers.

### Loading an Image from a URL

Only when you use the "load an image from a URL" feature, the request goes through our relay server, as follows:

- The URL you enter is sent to our relay server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers.
- The relay server fetches the image from that URL and streams it back to your browser. The image is then processed in your browser, just like with every other feature.
- The relay server does not store the fetched image or the URL you entered.
- However, to operate the Service and prevent abuse (for rate limiting), Cloudflare, Inc.'s systems may temporarily record access logs, including your IP address, the request URL, and your User-Agent.

Unless you use this feature, no information about your images is sent to our servers.

## Advertising

The Service uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To learn more about how Google uses information in advertising, see [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

To serve ads, scripts provided by third parties such as Google run on the Service's pages. Statements in this policy and on the Service that "images are not sent to a server" refer to our own servers. As described above, these ad scripts send cookies and browsing information to third-party ad vendors, but they do not send the images you load into the Service.

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we ask for your consent to the use of cookies and similar technologies through a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP). You can make or change your choices through the consent message shown on the site.

## Analytics

To understand how the Service is used and to improve it, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics.

- Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies.
- It collects aggregate information such as the URL of the pages you visit, the referring page, your browser and operating system type, and page load performance. We do not use it to identify individuals.
- For details on how Cloudflare handles information, see the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Information Stored on Your Device

The Service stores the following information in your browser on your device. None of it is sent to us.

- **Display preferences:** Your chosen theme and language are saved in your browser's localStorage. To keep them separate from other services on the same domain, the keys are prefixed with "image-adjust:".
- **The app itself:** So the Service can work offline, its program files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS, and so on) are stored using a Service Worker and Cache Storage.
- **Images shared from other apps:** When you share an image to the Service from another app, it's kept briefly in a cache on your device only until it's handed to the converter, and is deleted once the converter has received it.

You can clear all of this at any time by deleting the site's data in your browser settings.

## Personal Information

The Service has no user accounts, and you don't need to provide any personal information, such as your name or email address, to use it.

If you send us your name, email address, or a message through our contact form (Google Forms), we handle that information as follows:

- We use it only to respond to your inquiry and to improve the Service.
- We do not share it with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.
- We delete it in an appropriate manner once it is no longer needed.
- Information submitted through the contact form is stored on services provided by Google LLC.

If you'd like to access, correct, or delete your personal information, please contact us through the contact form. Once we've confirmed your identity, we'll respond promptly and within a reasonable scope.
