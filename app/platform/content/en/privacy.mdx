This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of this site ("we", "us", or "the Service") handles information about the people who use it.

## Advertising

The Service uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To learn more about how Google uses information in advertising, see [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

To serve ads, scripts provided by third parties such as Google run on the Service's pages. Statements in this policy and in each app that processing happens in your browser refer to our own servers. As described above, these ad scripts send cookies and browsing information to third-party ad vendors, but they do not send the files you process in an app.

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we ask for your consent to the use of cookies and similar technologies through a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP). You can make or change your choices through the consent message shown on the site.

## Analytics

To understand how the Service is used and to improve it, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics.

- Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies.
- It collects aggregate information such as the URL of the pages you visit, the referring page, your browser and operating system type, and page load performance. We do not use it to identify individuals.
- For details on how Cloudflare handles information, see the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Information Stored on Your Device

The Service stores the following information in your browser on your device. None of it is sent to us.

- **Display preferences:** Your chosen theme and language are saved in your browser's localStorage. To keep them separate from other services on the same domain, the keys are prefixed with "site:".

You can clear all of this at any time by deleting the site's data in your browser settings.

## Personal Information

The Service has no user accounts, and you don't need to provide any personal information, such as your name or email address, to use it.

We receive inquiries through [Issues](https://github.com/katoline/useful-site/issues) on the GitHub repository where the Service's source code is published. What you write in an issue, along with your account name, is **publicly visible to anyone**. Please don't include personal information such as your name, email address, or postal address.

- We use the content of issues only to respond to your inquiry and to improve the Service.
- Issues are stored on services provided by GitHub, Inc. and handled under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- If an issue accidentally contains personal information, we'll edit or delete it as soon as we notice.

If you have a privacy-related request that you'd rather not make public, just write "I'd like to contact you privately" in an issue (without any personal information), and we'll tell you how to reach us individually.
