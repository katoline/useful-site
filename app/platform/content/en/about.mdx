## About this site

<SiteName /> is a collection of small tools for everyday tasks — converting images, making PDFs, generating QR codes, measuring noise and checking your internet speed — that run entirely in your browser, with nothing to install and no account to create. Every tool is free.

The site exists to remove a familiar annoyance: having to install an unfamiliar app, or upload a private file to an unknown server, just to get one small job done.

## Our principles

### Your files stay on your device

Images, documents, QR code contents and similar inputs are processed on your device, inside your browser, and are not sent to our servers. Where a feature cannot work without a server (loading an image from a URL, or measuring connection speed), we say so on the screen and in the [privacy policy](/privacy/), and the server does not keep the data it receives.

### We are honest about how things work and their limits

Results depend on your device and browser. For example, the sound level meter gives an estimate based on your phone's microphone, and the speed test measures the connection to the nearest data center. Each tool's FAQ and guides explain how values are calculated and how far you can rely on them.

### Tools come with the knowledge to use them

Alongside each tool we publish guides — how to choose an image format, how to read a measurement, and so on — that help in the situations where you actually use the tool. You can find them under "Guide" in each tool's menu.

### Continuous improvement

Every tool is available in Japanese and English and is checked with automated tests on desktop and mobile layouts before release. We fix bugs, add features and update the guides on an ongoing basis; each guide shows when it was last updated.

## Advertising

To cover running costs, this site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Ads are placed so that they do not get in the way of the tools, and never next to file pickers or save buttons. For the cookies used by ads, see the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Contact

For feedback, feature requests, bug reports or any other enquiry, please open an [issue](https://github.com/katoline/useful-site/issues) in the GitHub repository where the site's source code is published.

Issues and your account name are **visible to everyone**. Please do not include personal information such as your name, email address or home address. If you need to discuss something privately, just write "I'd like to get in touch privately" in an issue and we will tell you how to reach us.

Depending on the content, it may take some time to reply, or we may not be able to reply. How we handle enquiries is described in the [privacy policy](/privacy/), and the conditions of use in the [terms & disclaimer](/terms/).
