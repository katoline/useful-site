Image Adjust is a tool that converts, compresses, and resizes images and generates web icons, all inside your browser. It is provided free of charge, and no account is required.

### Prohibited Use of the URL Relay

Abuse the URL relay feature, including sending large volumes of requests or bursts of requests in a short time, using it as a proxy to hide the source of requests to other sites, or using it to attack other websites or servers.

To keep Image Adjust stable and prevent abuse, features such as the URL relay are limited to a certain number of uses within a given period. If you reach the limit, please wait a while and try again. We may change these limits without notice.
