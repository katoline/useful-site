### How We Handle Your Images

All converting, compressing, and icon generation in Image Adjust happens inside your browser. The images you add, and the processed results, are never sent to or stored on our servers.

### Loading an Image from a URL

Only when you use the "load an image from a URL" feature, the request goes through our relay server, as follows:

- The URL you enter is sent to our relay server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers.
- The relay server fetches the image from that URL and streams it back to your browser. The image is then processed in your browser, just like with every other feature.
- The relay server does not store the fetched image or the URL you entered.
- However, to operate Image Adjust and prevent abuse (for rate limiting), Cloudflare, Inc.'s systems may temporarily record access logs, including your IP address, the request URL, and your User-Agent.

Unless you use this feature, no information about your images is sent to our servers.

### Information Stored on Your Device

- **The app itself:** So Image Adjust can work offline, its program files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS, and so on) are stored using a Service Worker and Cache Storage.
- **Images shared from other apps:** When you share an image to Image Adjust from another app, it's kept briefly in a cache on your device only until it's handed to the converter, and is deleted once the converter has received it.
