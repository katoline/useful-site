### How We Handle Your Documents

All Markdown conversion, previewing, and PDF saving in Markdown to PDF happens inside your browser. The documents you enter and the images you add are never sent to or stored on our servers. Images referenced by external URLs in your document are not loaded.

### Information Stored on Your Device

- **The document you are editing and your page setup:** So they survive a reload, they are kept temporarily in that tab's sessionStorage and are cleared when you close the tab. Images you add are not stored.
